How it works

  1. Offer Log in → Create offer → tick recipes you know. They appear on the Offers board.
  2. Demand Need something? Post a Demand. Crafters who know the recipe click Provide.
  3. Discord Request / Provide tags both players in the Market channel so you can trade mats.
